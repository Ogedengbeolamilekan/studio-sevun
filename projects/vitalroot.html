Selected work / Wellness

VitalRoot.

A warm, modern website that makes wellness services feel approachable and easy to navigate.

01 / Services

What went into it

The Wix website brings together welcoming wellness messaging, a clear introduction to the practice and responsive layouts designed for visitors on phones as well as desktops. The content is organized to help people understand the approach and find the information they need without friction.

02 / Design direction

Project highlights

Warm imagery, comfortable whitespace and a calm visual rhythm make the experience feel approachable. Clear section transitions and simple navigation support trust while giving the brand room to express its personality.
